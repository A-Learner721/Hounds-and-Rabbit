🐕 Hounds & Rabbit 🐰

A two-player hunt on one device.
Player 1 hunts with the hounds, Player 2 escapes with the rabbit.

How to play
  • Four hounds start in the board's corners; the rabbit starts in the centre. The hounds move first.
  • Each turn, the hound player moves one or two hounds. A hound moves 1 or 2 squares horizontally, vertically or diagonally — no L-shapes — and may never jump over another animal.
  • The rabbit moves to any square in the 5 × 5 area around it (its own square excepted). Only the rabbit may jump over hounds — but it can't land on one.
  • Five 🌳 trees sprout on random squares each game. No animal can move onto a tree or jump over one.
  • Every 20 moves the rabbit earns one burrow (they don't stack): it vanishes underground for two hound turns and reappears on any square it picked — the hounds only see the highlighted 5 × 5 zone around that spot. If a hound is sitting on the exact square when the rabbit resurfaces, the rabbit is dug out and the hounds win.
  • If a hound moves onto the rabbit's square, the hounds win. If the rabbit is still free when the move limit runs out, the rabbit wins.
  • No animal may leave the board. One "move" of the game length is one player's whole turn (a hound turn that moves two hounds still counts as one move).
